TITLE:
Trustbread: Sourdough Baking

SHORT DESCRIPTION:
Your ultimate guide to baking perfect sourdough bread at home.

LONG DESCRIPTION:
Master the art of artisanal baking with Trustbread!

Trustbread is the app designed for both beginners and experienced bakers who love sourdough. Forget complicated paper recipes and keep your entire process in the palm of your hand.

What will you find in Trustbread?
- Detailed Recipes: From classic country loaves to sourdough focaccias and bagels.
- Ingredient Checklist: Mark off what you have ready while preparing your dough.
- Educational Blog: Learn the science behind fermentation and the history of bread.
- Tool Guide: Discover essential tools to improve your crust and crumb.

Your perfect companion from the first feeding of your sourdough starter to the moment it leaves the oven. Start baking today!
