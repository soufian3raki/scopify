  - name: Ripe mashed banana
    amount: 250g
  - name: Sourdough discard
    amount: 150g
  - name: Sugar (or brown sugar)
    amount: 120g
  - name: Eggs (2 large)
    amount: 100g
  - name: Neutral oil
    amount: 80g
  - name: Plain flour
    amount: 220g
  - name: Baking soda
    amount: 5g
  - name: Salt + cinnamon
    amount: 5g
steps:
  - text: Whisk wet ingredients.
    time: ⏱ 10 min
    cookTitle: Wet Mix
    cookText: Whisk banana, discard, sugar, eggs and oil until smooth.
    cookTime: ⏱ 10 minutes
  - text: Incorporate dry ingredients.
    time: ⏱ 5 min
    cookTitle: Dry Mix
    cookText: Fold in flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon until just combined.
    cookTime: ⏱ 5 minutes
  - text: Pour into pan.
    time: ⏱ 2 min
    cookTitle: Pan
    cookText: Pour into a lined loaf tin and smooth the top.
    cookTime: ⏱ 2 minutes
  - text: Bake and cool.
    time: ⏱ 55 min
    cookTitle: Bake
    cookText: Bake at 175°C for 50–60 min. Rest in tin 10 min before slicing.
    cookTime: ⏱ 55 minutes

  - icon: 🍰

  - icon: 🥜
