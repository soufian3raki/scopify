    time: ⏱ 1 hour

    time: ⏱ 4 hours

  - text: Cold rest and shaping.
    time: ⏱ 12 hours

  - text: Score and bake with steam.
    time: ⏱ 22 min

    cookText: Make long diagonal slashes; bake at 250°C with heavy steam.
