times:
  prep: 45 min
  rest: 17h
  bake: 25 min
  total: 18h
yield: 2 ciabattas

ingredients:
  - name: Bread flour
    amount: 500g
  - name: Water (80%)
    amount: 400g
  - name: Active liquid starter
    amount: 100g
  - name: Olive oil
    amount: 20g
  - name: Fine salt
    amount: 10g
steps:
  - text: Autolyse flour and water.
    time: ⏱ 1 hour
    cookTitle: Autolyse
    cookText: Mix flour and 360 g water; rest 1 h to build hydration and strength.
    cookTime: ⏱ 1 hour
  - text: Develop with coil folds.
    time: ⏱ 2.5 hours
    cookTitle: Develop
    cookText: Add starter, salt, oil, and water. Perform 5 coil folds every 30 min.
    cookTime: ⏱ 2.5 hours
  - text: Bulk fermentation.
    time: ⏱ 2.5 hours
    cookTitle: Bulk
    cookText: Ferment at 24°C for 5 h total until very bubbly and jiggly.
    cookTime: ⏱ 2.5 hours
  - text: Divide gently.
    time: ⏱ 10 min
    cookTitle: Divide
    cookText: Turn onto floured surface; cut into 2 slippers without deflating the dough.
    cookTime: ⏱ 10 minutes
  - text: Bake with steam.
    time: ⏱ 25 min
    cookTitle: Bake
    cookText: Bake at 240°C with heavy steam for 22–25 min until crisp.
    cookTime: ⏱ 25 minutes
tips:
  - icon: 👟
    title: No Shaping
    text: Ciabatta's beauty is its irregular slipper shape — resist the urge to form it.
  - icon: 💨
    title: Steam
    text: Steam is vital for oven spring — use a cast iron pan with boiling water.
