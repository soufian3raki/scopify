steps:
  - text: Autolyse for extensibility.
    time: ⏱ 1.5 hours
    cookTitle: Autolyse
    cookText: Mix flours with most of the water and rest for 1.5 hours to build extensibility.
    cookTime: ⏱ 1.5 hours
  - text: Develop dough with coil folds.
    time: ⏱ 2.5 hours
    cookTitle: Develop
    cookText: Add starter, salt and remaining water. Use coil folds every 30 min, 4–5 times.
    cookTime: ⏱ 2.5 hours
  - text: Bulk fermentation.
    time: ⏱ 2.5 hours
    cookTitle: Bulk
    cookText: Ferment at 24–26°C until risen 50–75% with a domed, jiggly surface.
    cookTime: ⏱ 2.5 hours
  - text: Shape with high tension.
    time: ⏱ 20 min
    cookTitle: Shape
    cookText: Shape with high tension but a gentle hand; rest seam-up in a floured banneton.
    cookTime: ⏱ 20 minutes
  - text: Cold proof and bake.
    time: ⏱ 16 hours
    cookTitle: Bake
    cookText: "Chill 14–16 h. Bake in a screaming-hot Dutch oven: 20 min covered at 260°C, then 20–25 min open at 235°C."
    cookTime: ⏱ 16 hours
